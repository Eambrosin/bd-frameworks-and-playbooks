# Experience-Based Case — Solar Commercial Growth & International Sourcing

> **Experience-based case.** This case reflects Eduardo Ambrosin's work founding and commercially developing Seleto Solar.

## Context

Seleto Solar was developed in a market where commercial growth depended on more than selling photovoltaic systems.

The business needed to connect:

- customer acquisition
- solution positioning
- supplier selection
- equipment availability
- international sourcing
- pricing
- delivery confidence
- market education

The company was built during a period that included the COVID-era disruption to commercial activity and supply chains.

## Commercial Objective

Create a scalable solar sales operation capable of generating revenue while maintaining access to competitive photovoltaic equipment and suppliers.

## Actions

The commercial role included:

1. building the customer-acquisition and sales process;
2. developing B2B commercial relationships;
3. structuring proposals and commercial follow-up;
4. recruiting and developing the commercial team;
5. sourcing photovoltaic equipment internationally;
6. working primarily with suppliers in China while also evaluating alternatives in other markets;
7. aligning product availability, pricing and customer expectations;
8. supporting market education and external positioning around solar energy.

## Result

The business generated:

- **USD 1.8M in total revenue**;
- more than **USD 1M during the first 18 months**.

The experience also created direct exposure to international supplier relationships and the commercial impact of sourcing decisions on the final customer proposition.

## What This Demonstrates

The case combines:

- business development
- founder-led sales
- renewable-energy commercialization
- international sourcing
- supplier negotiation
- commercial team development
- customer education
- cross-border execution

## Lessons

### 1. Sourcing is part of the commercial proposition

Supplier reliability, lead time and equipment availability influence what the sales team can credibly promise.

### 2. Revenue growth and procurement cannot operate independently

A strong pipeline is valuable only if the company can deliver the solution under commercially acceptable conditions.

### 3. Market education can accelerate sales

In emerging or rapidly evolving categories, commercial conversations often require helping the customer understand the economics and operational implications of the solution.

### 4. International supplier relationships create both leverage and dependency

Price alone is not enough. Supplier quality, continuity, communication and logistics resilience matter.

Today I would formalize supplier and account intelligence in the same decision system so product availability, commercial attractiveness and customer timing are evaluated together.
