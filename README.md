## Core Operating Method

The frameworks in this repository follow a simple decision sequence:

```text
CONTEXT
What is happening?
      ↓
FIT
Does the opportunity match the commercial objective?
      ↓
VALUE
What is the potential commercial impact?
      ↓
RISK
What could reduce attractiveness or execution feasibility?
      ↓
ACTION
What should happen next?
```

The purpose is not to create complexity. It is to make commercial decisions **structured, transparent and repeatable**.

## Design Principles

### 1. Explain the decision

A score, recommendation or framework should be traceable to its underlying criteria.

### 2. Separate evidence from interpretation

Observed information, assumptions and commercial hypotheses should not be silently merged.

### 3. End with an action

Analysis is useful only when it helps determine what happens next.

### 4. Connect commercial and operational feasibility

An attractive opportunity that cannot be executed reliably is not automatically a good opportunity.

### 5. Keep human judgment in the loop

Frameworks and AI can improve consistency; they do not replace negotiation, due diligence, market validation or management judgment.

## Use & Limitations

This repository is a professional portfolio and decision-support library.

It does not replace:

- live market research
- legal or regulatory review
- financial due diligence
- procurement-specific requirements
- management approval
- organization-specific commercial policy

Examples and frameworks should be validated against the actual market, company and evidence available before real-world use.
