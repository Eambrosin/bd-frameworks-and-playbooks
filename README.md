# Purpose

Business Development often depends on judgment, experience and fragmented internal knowledge.

The objective of this repository is to convert that knowledge into **structured, repeatable and explainable commercial frameworks**.

The focus is on practical questions such as:

- Which opportunities deserve attention?
- Which accounts should be prioritized?
- Which partners offer the strongest strategic fit?
- What should happen before entering a new market?
- How should a company structure its GTM approach?
- Which commercial risks should be validated before allocating resources?
- What should the next Business Development action be?

The repository is designed to support human commercial judgment rather than replace it.

# Practical Business Development Playbook

A useful Business Development playbook should answer five questions.

## 1. What are we trying to achieve?

Define the commercial objective.

## 2. Who or what should we prioritize?

Identify the most relevant accounts, markets, partners or opportunities.

## 3. Why does the opportunity matter?

Define the strategic and commercial rationale.

## 4. How should we approach it?

Determine channel, GTM motion, partnership model or engagement strategy.

## 5. What happens next?

Translate analysis into a clear commercial action.

| Commercial Value | What is the potential economic value? |

# 90-Day Market Entry Framework

A practical early-stage international expansion plan can be organized into three phases.

## Days 1–30 — Validate

Focus on:

- Market assumptions
- Target segment
- Customer profile
- Competition
- Pricing
- Regulatory considerations
- Potential partners
- Route-to-market hypotheses

## Days 31–60 — Engage

Focus on:

- Prospect conversations
- Partner outreach
- Commercial validation
- Market feedback
- Channel assessment
- Stakeholder mapping
- Local relationship development

## Days 61–90 — Execute

Focus on:

- Prioritized commercial opportunities
- Partnership negotiations
- GTM refinement
- Local commercial process
- Pipeline creation
- Market-entry milestones
- Next-stage investment decision

# Evidence-Aware Commercial Analysis

Commercial recommendations should distinguish between four categories.

## Known Information

Facts supported by available data.

## Commercial Interpretation

A conclusion derived from structured analysis.

## Hypothesis

An idea that may be commercially relevant but still requires validation.

## Unknown Information

Information that should be investigated before making a decision.

This distinction becomes especially important when AI is involved.

Unverified information should not be presented as fact.

# Guiding Principles

## 1. Commercial Logic Before Technology

Technology should support the commercial process, not define it.

## 2. Explainability Matters

A recommendation should be understandable.

## 3. Prioritization Requires Trade-Offs

Not every opportunity deserves the same level of attention.

## 4. Strategy Must Lead to Action

Commercial analysis should produce a clear next step.

## 5. International Growth Requires Context

Commercial approaches should reflect differences between markets, customers and business environments.

## 6. AI Should Support Human Judgment

AI is most useful when combined with structured commercial thinking and real-world Business Development experience.
