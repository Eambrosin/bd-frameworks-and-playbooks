# Partnership Evaluation Framework

## Objective

Provide a structured methodology to evaluate potential strategic partners before entering a commercial relationship.

## Evaluation Criteria

| Category               | Weight |
| ---------------------- | ------ |
| Strategic Alignment    | 25%    |
| Market Access          | 20%    |
| Revenue Potential      | 20%    |
| Operational Capability | 15%    |
| Financial Stability    | 10%    |
| Cultural Fit           | 10%    |

## Scoring Method

Each category receives a score from 1 to 10.

Final Score:

Final Score = Σ (Score × Weight)

## Example

| Criteria               | Score |
| ---------------------- | ----- |
| Strategic Alignment    | 9     |
| Market Access          | 8     |
| Revenue Potential      | 8     |
| Operational Capability | 7     |
| Financial Stability    | 9     |
| Cultural Fit           | 8     |

Overall Partnership Score: 8.2/10

## Recommendation Thresholds

* 9.0 – 10.0 → Strategic Priority Partner
* 8.0 – 8.9 → High Potential Partner
* 7.0 – 7.9 → Selective Opportunity
* Below 7.0 → Not Recommended
