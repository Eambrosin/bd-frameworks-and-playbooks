# 90-Day Market Entry Playbook

A practical early-stage framework for validating a new market before committing to full-scale expansion.

## Principle

The first 90 days should reduce uncertainty, create commercial evidence and produce a clear next investment decision.

```text
VALIDATE → ENGAGE → EXECUTE → DECIDE
```

---

## Days 1–30 — Validate

### Objectives

- define the target segment
- test the market thesis
- identify regulatory/commercial constraints
- map customers, partners and competitors
- establish a preliminary route-to-market hypothesis

### Actions

- [ ] define ICP / target-account profile
- [ ] identify priority geographies
- [ ] map 25–100 target accounts depending on market structure
- [ ] identify 10–20 potential partners / channels where relevant
- [ ] review pricing and positioning
- [ ] identify regulatory and legal questions requiring specialist confirmation
- [ ] interview market participants where possible
- [ ] define success criteria for the next 60 days

### Deliverables

- market thesis
- account universe
- partner universe
- competitor map
- entry-risk register
- preliminary value proposition
- first route-to-market hypothesis

### Decision Gate

**Is there enough commercial evidence to invest in direct market engagement?**

---

## Days 31–60 — Engage

### Objectives

- replace desk research with real market feedback
- validate customer pain / opportunity
- validate partner interest
- test messaging and route-to-market assumptions

### Actions

- [ ] prioritize target accounts
- [ ] launch qualification-first outreach
- [ ] run customer discovery conversations
- [ ] approach potential distributors / partners
- [ ] record objections and recurring buying criteria
- [ ] refine value proposition
- [ ] test local channel and communication assumptions
- [ ] identify decision-makers and buying process
- [ ] update the risk register

### Deliverables

- qualified-account shortlist
- partner shortlist
- customer feedback summary
- revised positioning
- initial pipeline
- evidence-based commercial objections / requirements

### Decision Gate

**Are customers or partners showing enough concrete relevance to justify execution investment?**

---

## Days 61–90 — Execute

### Objectives

- convert evidence into the first repeatable commercial motion
- advance the strongest opportunities
- define the operating model required for scale

### Actions

- [ ] focus on highest-quality accounts
- [ ] progress discovery to proposal / pilot where justified
- [ ] run partner due diligence
- [ ] negotiate pilot / representation / channel terms where relevant
- [ ] define local support requirements
- [ ] validate pricing and unit economics
- [ ] define CRM / reporting process
- [ ] identify hires, contractors or local resources required
- [ ] prepare next-stage investment plan

### Deliverables

- active qualified pipeline
- pilot / proposal opportunities
- selected partner candidates
- 6–12 month GTM plan
- commercial resource plan
- scale / pause recommendation

### Final Decision

Choose one:

```text
SCALE
CONTINUE VALIDATION
PIVOT ENTRY MODEL
PAUSE / DO NOT ENTER
```

The decision should be supported by observed evidence, not only by market-size statistics.
